# Task 0, version B: credit usage of a tiered-refresh NBA props feed

**Run:** Nov 20, 2026, 00:10 to 01:09 UTC (Nov 19, 6:10 to 7:09 PM Central) · **Author:** Alex Le · **Data:** `src/live_feed/runs/version_b/20261120T001000Z/` (local, not in git)

## Short answer

- Keeping 3 prop markets of 1 NBA game live for the **last hour before tip-off** cost **108 credits** for **1,944 odds changes**.
- Credits depend only on **polls × markets × regions**, never on how much the odds move. Each poll cost exactly 3 credits.
- **Monthly, NBA only (about 220 games), props offered 24 h before tip-off:** 50 markets cost about **1.4M credits**, so the **5M plan ($119)**, the plan chosen, covers it with about 3.6M credits to spare for other sports.
- Version B costs less than version A's flat 2-minute polling whenever a game is covered for more than about 1.25 hours. It is also faster close to tip-off (median delay 31 s vs 35 s).

The run started exactly 60 minutes before tip-off, so the 15-minute interval did not run. Its cost is still predictable: 3 credits per poll, 4 polls per hour.

| Polls with no price change | 0 | 3 | 3 of 35 (the first poll is the baseline) |

- **Changes by kind:** 1,734 price-only, 210 line moves.
- **Changes by market:** points 831, rebounds 715, assists 398.
- **Changes by book:** FanDuel 564, DraftKings 457, Bovada 284, BetOnline 262, BetRivers 193, BetMGM 184.

The change count is net: a price that moved twice between two polls counts once. With 5-minute refreshes, some movement is never seen.

## Latency (bonus)

The API doesn't say when a book changed a price. The closest timestamp is the market's `last_update`, which is when The Odds API last read the book.

| From → to | Interval | Median | 90th percentile | Max | Samples |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| API `last_update` → received by the feed | 5 min | 51 s | 133 s | 160 s | 579 changes |
| API `last_update` → received by the feed | 1 min | 31 s | 54 s | 84 s | 1,365 changes |
| API `last_update` → loaded by the props dashboard | all | 22 s | | 65 s | 35 pulls |
| Received by the feed → loaded by the dashboard | all | 1.0 s | | 2.0 s | 35 pulls |

- **Where the delay comes from:** almost all of it is the poll interval. A 1-minute refresh cuts the typical delay from about 50 s to about 30 s and keeps the worst case under 1.5 minutes.
- **Dashboard row:** its median is lower than the feed's because the dashboard compares against the newest `last_update` in each pull, while the feed row uses each changed price's own market.
- **Missing sample:** the dashboard started after the first poll, so 35 of the 36 pulls are logged.

## Monthly estimate (to choose a tier)

Polls per game for `H` hours of pre-game coverage (H ≥ 1) = 15-min polls for the first H − 1 hours, then 6 polls at 5 min and 30 at 1 min = **4 × (H − 1) + 36**. Credits = polls × markets × regions.

Assumptions (not measured, same as version A): about **220 NBA games a month**, 1 region (`us`), no props during the game.

| Markets per game | Hours per game | Credits per game | **Version B credits/month** | Version A, plain 2-min polling |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 3 | 1 | 108 | 23,760 | 19,800 |
| 3 | 2 | 120 | **26,400** | 39,600 |
| 3 | 6 | 168 | **36,960** | 118,800 |
| 5 | 6 | 280 | 61,600 | 198,000 |
| 8 | 6 | 448 | 98,560 | 316,800 |
| 10 | 2 | 400 | 88,000 | 132,000 |
| 10 | 6 | 560 | 123,200 | 396,000 |
| 3 | 24 | 384 | 84,480 | 475,200 |
| 8 | 24 | 1,024 | 225,280 | 1,267,200 |
| 50 | 24 | 6,400 | **1,408,000** | 7,920,000 |

**Plan chosen: 5M ($119), with props offered at least 24 h before tip-off.** That's 128 polls per game (92 at 15 min, 6 at 5 min, 30 at 1 min). For NBA alone, 5M covers up to about 177 markets per game (5,000,000 ÷ (128 × 220)). 50 NBA markets use about 28% of it, leaving about 3.6M for tennis, soccer or a second region.

Plans on Nov 17, 2026, from version A's report ([the-odds-api.com](https://the-odds-api.com/#get-access)): Free 500 · 20K $30 · 100K $59 · 5M $119 · 15M $249.

- **NBA props only, up to about 8 markets, 6 hours before tip-off:** **100K plan ($59)**.
- **More markets, a second region, live in-game props, or tennis and soccer on top:** **5M plan ($119)**. It leaves plenty of room.

## Version B vs version A

| | Version A (`/markets` check, every 2 min) | Version B (1 / 5 / 15 min by time to tip-off) |
|---|---|---|
| Credits for 1 hour, 3 markets | 120 | 108 (last hour before tip-off) |
| Credits for 6 hours, 3 markets | 720 | 168 |
| Median delay | 35 s | 31 s in the last 30 min, 51 s from 60 to 30 min |
| When it's tight | the same all day | where bets come in: close to tip-off |

Version B spends credits where odds move most: 1,365 changes in the last 30 minutes, against 627 in a full hour of version A run 3 hours before tip-off.

## Limits of this test

- One game and one hour. The 15-minute interval's cost is calculated, not measured.
- The monthly figures assume 220 games and the same 3-credits-per-poll cost per 3 markets. Markets a book doesn't offer cost nothing, so the real cost can be a little lower.
- Odds changes are net between polls, so fast-moving prices are undercounted at 5 and 15 minutes.
- Players are matched by name (`description`). Production needs a player mapping table.
